Krill Bot

Terms

Krill Bot is an unofficial Discord companion for Krillion, made by TheScottBot. It is not made, endorsed or run by Krillion or its maker. The game itself is at https://krillion.io.

Who can use it

Krill Bot is private. It runs only on servers its author has added it to, and anyone in those servers may use its commands.

Using it fairly

  • Log only your own results. The Log Krillion result menu refuses anyone else's message, and boards are only as honest as the results people log.
  • Follow Discord's terms and Krillion's own rules while using it.
  • Do not use Krill Bot to spoil a day's prompts or answers for anyone.

What it provides

Krill Bot is provided as it is, without any promise that it will be available, correct or kept running. Boards and streaks are worked out from the results players choose to log, and may not match Krillion's own records. The author may change, pause or stop it at any time.
