Krill Bot

An unofficial Discord companion for Krillion.

Krill Bot keeps a server's own Krillion scoreboard. Players log the result Krillion's Copy result button gives them, and the bot keeps daily and rolling boards, streaks, and an optional daily post with yesterday's board.

It is not made, endorsed or run by Krillion or its maker, and it never contacts krillion.io. Play the game at https://krillion.io.

Krill Bot is private: it runs only on servers its author has added it to.

Commands

  • /dive log, or Log Krillion result on your own message: record a result
  • /dive today, /dive board, /dive streak, /dive me: boards, streaks and your own results
  • /dive forget: delete your results, after you confirm
  • /setup: server settings and the daily post (needs Manage Server)
  • /help: what the bot does, in Discord
